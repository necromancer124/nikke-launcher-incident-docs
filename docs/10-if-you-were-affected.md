# 10. If you were affected

A short checklist. The publisher's own instructions come first; this is what I'd tell a friend.

**Who should check:** anyone who opened the NIKKE **Windows PC launcher** at least once since **September 27, 2026**.

## Do this, in this order

1. **Don't open the launcher** until you've checked.
2. **Save your logs first.** Copy the launcher's `log` and `intl_service` folders to a USB stick or another drive. Cleanup tools delete them, and they are your only proof.
3. **Check.** Use the publisher's official check, and/or a community check kit. Read any tool before running it.
   - Official instructions: `[TODO: link to the Level Infinite notice]`
   - Springz's step-by-step guide: https://github.com/SpringZali/nikke-launcher-triage
4. **Clean** as the official guide says.
5. **From a different device:** change your passwords (email first, then money, then the rest), log out of all sessions, turn on two-factor authentication.
6. **Watch your accounts** for a few weeks: logins from places you don't know, password-reset emails you didn't ask for, payments you didn't make.
7. **Report it.**

## Reporting matters more than you think

As of **November 13, 2026**, as far as I know, the attacker has not been caught.

- **Report to your local police or national cybercrime authority.** Even if you lost nothing. One report is a statistic; thousands of reports from many countries are a case.
- **Report losses to the publisher** through the official channel.
- **Tell your bank** immediately if money is involved.
- Keep your saved logs. They are evidence.

It is in the guides, but it deserves saying on its own: reporting is **more** important than which cleanup tool you pick. Tools fix your PC. Reports are how someone eventually gets caught.

## Don't

- Don't run random "NIKKE virus cleaners" from strangers. Read the code or use a source you can verify.
- Don't visit the attacker's domain "to see what's there".
- Don't harass the hosting company or the registrar. They are not the attacker, and they acted quickly.
- Don't assume a clean antivirus scan means you were fine. Mine found nothing on the infected laptop.
- Don't panic. Be methodical.

## If you only do one thing

Save your logs, then change your passwords from another device.
